About

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Control Science and Engineering with a background in Computer Science. My work spans deep learning, structured-data modeling, and biomedical engineering. I am especially interested in learning robust representations from heterogeneous data and evaluating predictive models in scientifically meaningful ways.

I am currently involved in tabular foundation model (LimiX) research with Tsinghua University and Wenzhun Intelligence, and have previously contributed to AI projects involving Ximo Technology and Zhujian Intelligence.

Research Interests

Tabular Foundation Models

In-context learning, model architectures, mixture-of-experts, and generalization across structured datasets.

Ophthalmic AI & Multimodal Learning

Fundus photographs, glaucoma visual-field forecasting, and longitudinal clinical data fusion.

Physiological Signal Modeling

PPG-based blood pressure estimation, quantile regression, and predictive uncertainty.

Digital Eye Simulation & AI for Science

Digital simulated eyes, whole-eye OCT reconstruction, ocular digital twins, and metrology-oriented model validation.
