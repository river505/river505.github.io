About

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Control Science and Engineering with a background in Computer Science. My work spans deep learning, structured-data modeling, and biomedical engineering. I am especially interested in learning robust representations from heterogeneous data and evaluating predictive models in scientifically meaningful ways.

My experience also includes applied AI work and technical exchanges involving teams at Wenzhun Intelligence (稳准智能), Ximo Technology (硒钼科技), and Emotibot / Zhujian Intelligence (竹间智能), connecting research ideas with practical engineering problems.

Research Interests

Tabular Foundation Models

Ophthalmic AI & Multimodal Learning

Physiological Signal Modeling

AI for Science & Measurement

Scientific machine learning, computational eye modeling, benchmarking, and measurement reliability.
